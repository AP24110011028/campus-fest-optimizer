Capstone Notes

Why this is stronger

The project now solves three linked planning layers: funding, single-stage value, and lane allocation. That makes the algorithm choice feel justified instead of decorative.

What to say in viva

Use greedy when the problem has a proven greedy-choice property or when you want a fast baseline. Use dynamic programming when local choices can block the optimal global result.

Complexity snapshot

Knapsack runs in O(nB), weighted interval scheduling runs in O(n log n), and the greedy baselines run in O(n log n) because of sorting.

If someone has a doubt

Why are some event cards faded?

They are filtered out by the current category selection or minimum priority. They are not part of the current optimization input.

What should I do first if I am confused?

Start with a preset, then click Recompute Plan, then read the four main result cards from left to right.

Why is DP sometimes better than greedy?

Greedy makes locally good choices fast, but DP explores combinations across budget, staff, and equipment, so it can recover better global trade-offs.

When should I check the trace tables?

Use them when you want to explain exactly how the optimizer reached its answer during viva or review.

What if I want to test my own idea?

Add a custom event in the left panel, then recompute. The app will automatically include it in future analysis and save it locally.

What if I do not know what to say next in presentation?

Open the recommendations panel and the exported report. Both are designed to give you ready-made talking points.
